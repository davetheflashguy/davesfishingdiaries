Getting Started: Freshwater Fishing in New Jersey

Simple steps, responsible practices, and community impact

Freshwater fishing in New Jersey is a rewarding way to connect with nature, unwind, and create lasting memories. Whether you’re casting your first line or returning to a beloved hobby, here are some key things to know before you go.

Licensing — Know Before You Go

In New Jersey, a valid fishing license is required for most freshwater fishing. Licenses fund conservation, habitat restoration, and enforcement that protect our waterways. For details on how to obtain a license, visit the New Jersey Division of Fish & Wildlife: NJ Fish & Wildlife.

Why Licenses Matter

  • Support fish population management and stocking programs
  • Pay for habitat restoration and public access improvements
  • Fund education, enforcement, and angler services

Be a Positive Role Model

Lead by example: practice catch-and-release when appropriate, pick up litter, follow regulations, and teach respectful stewardship. Small actions help keep waterways healthy for everyone.

Benefits of Taking a Kid Fishing

  • Builds confidence, patience, and curiosity
  • Creates lasting memories and stronger family bonds
  • Introduces youth to conservation and outdoor skills

Quick Checklist

  • Valid NJ fishing license
  • Appropriate rod, reel, and tackle
  • Life jacket for kids near water
  • Sun protection and water
  • Respect posted rules and private property

Things to Avoid

  • Technological distractions
  • Drugs and alcohol - Visit NNJAA for resources
  • Tresspassing
